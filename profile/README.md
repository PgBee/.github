## About PgBee

PgBee connects students with **verified hostels and PGs**, and gives owners the tools to manage tenants and rent without the paperwork. Our mission is to remove misinformation from student housing and make finding, booking and living in a PG simple and secure.

PgBee is made up of two apps that work together:

| App | Who it's for | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PgBee** | Students | Find, book and live in a verified PG |
| **PgBee Owners** | PG and hostel owners | Manage tenants, rooms and rent |

---

## 🎓 PgBee (Student App)

Everything a student needs, from the first search to the monthly rent.

**Before booking**
- Search by location, budget and amenities, with filters for room type, distance and more
- Verified listings with photos, amenities, reviews, map and owner contact
- Wishlist to save and compare stays
- Secure booking

**After booking**
- Dashboard with rent due, due date and one-tap **Pay Now**
- **My Stay** with booking details, house rules and owner contact
- Rent history and receipts
- **Request an amenity** (WiFi, AC, cleaning and more) and track its status
- **Raise a ticket** to PgBee support for complaints and disputes
- **Promo codes** and referral offers
- Request a rent extension by proposing a new due date
- Inbox for owner messages, reminders and ticket updates

---

## 🏠 PgBee Owners (Owner App)

A simple management console for PG and hostel owners.

- **Owner dashboard:** active tenants, pending rent, overdue students and extension requests, with filters
- **Room and bed allocation:** linked to the right hostel, with no duplicate allocations
- **Tenant management:** tenant changes stay pending until PgBee Admin approves
- **Monthly rent ledger:** billing cycle and due date per student, with statuses paid, unpaid, partially paid and overdue
- **Rent reminders:** send reminders and keep a reminder history
- **Payment communication:** structured in-app requests for payment reports, incorrect-amount queries, verification and extra time
- **Extension requests:** approve, reject or suggest another date, with the full decision history kept

---

## 🔐 Verification

Every listing on PgBee is checked by the PgBee team before it goes live. Owners are onboarded and linked to their hostels by PgBee Admin, so what students see is what they get.

---

## 🛠️ Tech and Infrastructure

- Web frontends deployed on **Cloudflare Pages**
- Backend, database and secrets hosted on a PgBee-controlled **AWS** account over HTTPS
- A PgBee Admin console handles onboarding, approvals and verification

> Mobile apps, WhatsApp/SMS reminders, real-time chat and wider payment integrations are planned separately.
