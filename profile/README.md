# 🐝 PgBee – Simplifying Student Accommodations

Welcome to **PgBee**, an innovative platform that connects students with **verified hostels and PGs**.  
Our mission is to eliminate misinformation, provide transparency, and make student housing simple, secure, and hassle-free.

---

## 🌍 Our Mission
To empower students by providing **trustworthy accommodations** and to support PG/Hostel owners with **streamlined management tools**.

---

## 🚀 What We Do

### ✅ Verified Listings
- Every PG and hostel on PgBee is **verified for authenticity**.  
- Students get **transparent details** with photos, amenities, and reviews.  

### 🔍 Effortless Search & Booking
- Advanced filters for **location, budget, and amenities**.  
- One-click secure booking.  

### 💳 Seamless Rent & Bill Management
- Online rent payments, integrated billing for water, electricity, and utilities.  
- Reduces hassle for both students and PG owners.  

---

## 🛠️ Our Projects
PgBee is built with **modern web technologies** to ensure speed, security, and scalability.  
Some of our repos you’ll find here:
